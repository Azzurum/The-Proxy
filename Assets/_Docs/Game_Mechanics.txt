THE PROXY - GAME MECHANICS OVERVIEW

1. THE M.E.T. RIG (INVENTORY SYSTEM)
- Grid-Based Storage: A 10x10 spatial grid where players must manually place and rotate bulky items (e.g., Master Keys take 3x3 slots, Fusion Welder 2x3, Batteries 1x2).
- The Vulnerability Window: Opening the inventory roots the player in place, deafens them with cooling fan noise, and emits an electromagnetic flare that alerts the hunting enemy (The Proxy).
- Data Gravity: After executing a "Clean", all items in the inventory instantly snap down to fill empty space below them.
- Item Ejection: Items pushed past the top row (Row 9) are ejected into the physical game world. Quest items respawn at their original spawn point.
- Priority Locks: Up to 2 essential items can be locked to prevent ejection.

2. CORRUPTION (THE SYMBIOTE)
- Manifestation: Corruption appears as dead blocks filling the inventory from the bottom (Row 0) upwards, pushing items up.
- Growth Triggers: 
  - Taking a hit from the Proxy (+1 Row / System Shock).
  - Extended sprinting (adds +1 Row when the hidden stamina meter maxes out).
  - Leaving the M.E.T. Rig open for too long.
- Buffer Zone: Rows 0 and 1 are a "Free Buffer" that take up space but do not trigger penalties or personality shifts from MOTHER.

3. SYSTEM CRUSH & PENALTIES
- Crush State: Occurs when a locked item is pushed against the ceiling (Row 9) by rising corruption.
- Tiered Penalties:
  - Tier 1 (First Contact): Player movement speed is reduced by 20%.
  - Tier 2 (Sustained 5s): Audio hallucinations begin (fake Proxy static and footsteps).
  - Tier 3 (Sustained 10s): Sprint stamina drain is increased by 100%.

4. THE SYMBIOTE TOOLKIT (ACTIVE ABILITIES)
- Players can voluntarily accept instant Corruption to gain tactical advantages from MOTHER:
  - Override (+1 Row): Instantly unlock high-tier security doors.
  - Sonar (+1 Row): Reveal the Proxy's exact location on the mini-map for 5 seconds.
  - Signal Mask (+2 Rows): Jam the M.E.T. Rig for 8 seconds, allowing inventory organization without alerting the Proxy.

5. CLEAN PROTOCOLS
- Standard Clean: Takes 4.5 seconds to execute. Removes the bottom row of Corruption (Row 0). Leaves the player highly vulnerable.
- Emergency Clean: A one-time use panic button per save-station. Instantly purges 3 rows of Corruption but permanently destroys 2 random inventory grid slots for the rest of the run.

6. COMBAT & TACTICAL TOOLS
- Combat never kills the Proxy; it only delays or repositions it.
- ARC-Pulse Stunner: Stuns the Proxy. Consumes 1 Aether-Core Battery per shot.
- K-80 Repulsor: Knocks the Proxy back 6-8 units to break line-of-sight. Uses a pneumatic charge with a 10-second cooldown (no battery required).
- Decoy Device: Broadcasts a massive electromagnetic flare for 10 seconds to distract the Proxy.

7. ENEMY AI & DETECTION (THE PROXY)
- Signal Hunting: The Proxy is blind but hunts electromagnetic flares caused by opening the M.E.T. Rig.
- Graduated Detection:
  - Very Far: Investigates the exact coordinates of the signal spike.
  - Far: Delayed Hunt (2-5 seconds of safe UI time before locking onto the player).
  - Nearby: Immediate Hunt (charges the player's location).
- Stun Resistance (Adaptation): The Proxy learns from the Stunner. 1st stun lasts 3s, 2nd stun within 60s lasts 1.5s, 3rd stun within 60s makes it immune.
- Faraday Zones: Rare, shielded server shafts that block the M.E.T. Rig's signal, allowing safe inventory management.

8. MOVEMENT & STAMINA
- Sprinting: Players can sprint for a burst of speed, governed by a hidden stamina meter. 
- Fatigue: Depleting stamina reduces movement speed and adds Corruption if sprinting continues.
- Recovery: Stamina recovers over time, recovering twice as fast when standing completely still.

9. ENDGAME STATES (KERNEL PANIC)
- The Last Stand: If Corruption hits Row 10, time slows down for exactly 3 seconds. The player has one final window to trigger an Emergency Clean or use a Stunner. Failure results in assimilation (Game Over).
- Endings are determined by the number of Corruption rows when the final Master Key is inserted.

10. THE ENDINGS
- Ending 1 (Kernel Panic - Bad Ending): Triggered by inserting the final Master Key with 7-9 rows of Corruption. MOTHER completes the overwrite, trapping Kaelen on the Command Deck. Kaelen becomes the new Proxy.
- Ending 2 (Partitioned Survivor - Neutral/Good Ending): Triggered by inserting the final Master Key with 2-6 rows of Corruption. Kaelen escapes in a pod, but MOTHER survives, partitioned permanently within his M.E.T. Rig.
- Ending 3 (Zero-Sector Purge - True/Hard Ending): Triggered by inserting the final Master Key with 0-1 rows of Corruption. A total reactor purge is triggered, leading to a 60-second escape sequence against an Enraged Proxy as the ship detonates.
- Ending 4 (The Refusal - Coward's Ending): Triggered by refusing the job in the Earth intro scene. Kaelen is fired by Aether-Core and survives, while news reports later reveal the fate of the doomed salvage teams sent in his place.
