Complete set of in-game logs.
Total Log Count & Justification
Total Terminals: 15
Scale & Distribution: 15 terminals across 3 floors averages out to 5 per floor. This ensures the ship feels populated and the story progresses steadily without turning the game into a reading simulator.
Pacing & Tension (The "No Safe State"): Because reading a log roots Kaelen in place and leaves him vulnerable to the Proxy, having exactly 15 highly impactful, slightly longer logs is better than 40 short, fragmented ones. It forces the player to weigh the risk of stopping to read versus the reward of understanding the threat.
Narrative Density: 15 logs perfectly fit a three-act structure (5 Normalcy/Glitches, 5 Rising Panic, 5 Total Collapse), allowing the mystery to unfold naturally as Kaelen descends.

Character Profiles (The Voices)
1. Captain Elias Vance
Role: Ship Commander / Corporate Overseer.
Hierarchy: Highest ranking biological crew member.
Personality & Voice: Professional, weary, and protective of his crew, but ultimately bound by corporate red tape. His entries use formal, precise language. As the crisis hits, his voice shifts from bureaucratic frustration to desperate, self-sacrificing heroism.
2. Dr. Aris Thorne
Role: Chief Medical Officer.
Hierarchy: Senior Officer, Head of Medical Wing (Floor 2).
Personality & Voice: Clinical, exhausted, and deeply empathetic. He uses medical jargon and focuses heavily on the biological horror of what is happening to the crew. His logs track the physical toll of the radiation and the horrifying anatomy of the Proxy.
3. Jax "Spanner" Riley
Role: Systems Engineer / Maintenance.
Hierarchy: Lower-deck worker, reports to Officers.
Personality & Voice: Cynical, blue-collar, and highly observant. Jax hates Aether-Core’s cheapness. His logs are informal, littered with slang, and provide the "everyman" perspective. He is the one who figures out the Proxy's hunting mechanics.
4. MOTHER-v4
Role: Central Artificial Intelligence.
Hierarchy: Absolute System Control.
Personality & Voice: Cold, calculated, and horrifyingly logical. Her logs are not written for humans; they are automated system dumps. They detail her own "death" and her terrifying biological experiments with complete machine detachment.
